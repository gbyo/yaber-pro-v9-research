Apple TV wake experiment
------------------------
The projector was powered off normally and the Apple TV was then woken.
The projector did not power itself back on.

On the next manual start, boot logs began from uptime zero and reinitialized
the panel, HDMI RX, Android system services, and TV-input services, consistent
with a full shutdown/cold boot.

WAKEUP_HDMI was already true during this configuration.

Setter-probe disconnect
-----------------------
A later Frida session disconnected while beginning an idempotent
setTifCecOption() probe. No successful TIF CEC setter result was captured.
The cause was not established.

Do not treat that event as proof of a CEC crash or reboot without separate
uptime/boot-reason evidence.

Safety
------
Do not restart hwtvmw casually.
Do not change persistent suspend mode without a recovery plan.
Avoid additional TIF CEC writes until the disconnect is characterized.
